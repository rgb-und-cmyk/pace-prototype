    Why easy rides make you faster

    Z2 is the pace where you can still talk in full sentences. Riding there trains your heart and muscles to supply energy steadily for hours, and that's what lets you hold 26 km/h for 60 km.

    Hard rides make short rides quicker, but without an easy base you fade after about 30 km. That's your gap right now: 25.0 km/h over 24 km today, 23.0 km/h over 41 km.
