    Why easy rides make you faster

    Z2 is the pace where you can still talk in full sentences. It trains your heart and muscles to supply energy steadily for hours, which is what holding 26 km/h for 60 km takes.

    Hard rides make short rides quicker, but without an easy base you fade after about 30 km: 25.0 km/h over 24 km today, 23.0 km/h over 41 km.
