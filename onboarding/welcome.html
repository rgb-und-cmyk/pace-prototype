Pace

Ride with a goal. Pace reads every ride against it.

Garmin and Strava show you what happened. Pace tells you if it got you closer to your goal, and what to do on your next ride.

  1. Set your goalA group ride, a distance, or riding more often. About 2 minutes.
  2. Connect your ridesFrom Garmin Connect or Strava. You keep riding as usual.
  3. See your last ride, read against your goalThen one clear next step.
